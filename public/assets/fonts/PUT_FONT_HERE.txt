FONT GOES IN THIS FOLDER
========================

    font.ttf

That exact filename. It's already wired up in index.html and will be used
for the menu, HUD and all in-game text.

No font.ttf? Falls back to Courier New. Nothing breaks.

.ttf and .otf both work (keep the .ttf filename either way).
For .woff2, change the @font-face format() in index.html to "woff2".

Free fonts that suit this game:
    https://fonts.google.com          try Special Elite, VT323, Share Tech Mono
    https://www.dafont.com            check the licence before shipping
    https://fontsquirrel.com          filtered to commercial-use-OK

CHECK THE LICENCE if you ever sell the game. "Free for personal use"
is NOT the same as free.
