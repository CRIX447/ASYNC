TEXTURES GO IN THIS FOLDER
==========================

Expected filenames (all optional):

    wall.jpg       yellow wallpaper, seamless
    carpet.jpg     damp mustard carpet, seamless
    ceiling.jpg    office ceiling tile, seamless

MUST be seamless / tileable or you'll see obvious grid lines on every wall.
1024x1024 or 2048x2048 is plenty. Bigger just costs load time.

Register them (or add new ones) in:  src/config/manifest.js

Free CC0 textures:
    https://ambientcg.com     search "wallpaper", "carpet", "concrete"
    https://polyhaven.com

Missing files fall back to generated placeholders, so the game still runs.
