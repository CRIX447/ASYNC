SOUNDS GO IN THIS FOLDER
========================
Format: .mp3 (or .ogg). All optional -- missing files are simply skipped.

START WITH THESE FOUR. They are most of the atmosphere:
    hum.mp3            fluorescent buzz, LOOPING
    footstep.mp3       single step
    drone.mp3          low ambient bed, LOOPING
    monster_idle.mp3   its breathing, LOOPING, volume tracks distance

THE MONSTER
    monster_idle.mp3   LOOPING. Gets louder as it closes in.
    monster_step.mp3   its footsteps
    monster_alert.mp3  the moment it spots you  <- make this nasty
    monster_chase.mp3  LOOPING, plays while hunting you
    monster_lost.mp3   it loses track of you
    jumpscare.mp3      it catches you  <- loud, short, no long tail

PLAYER
    jump.mp3           breath out
    land.mp3           impact
    breathe.mp3        LOOPING (reserved)
    hide_in.mp3        squeezing into a crack

INTERFACE / WORLD
    menu.mp3           main menu, LOOPING
    click.mp3          flashlight + switch
    error.mp3          wrong switch order
    door.mp3           exit opening
    heartbeat.mp3      LOOPING, below 25% sanity

RANDOM DISTANT EVENTS (fire every 20-55s, more often as sanity drops)
    far_thump.mp3      far_creak.mp3
    far_voice.mp3      buzz_pop.mp3

LOOPS MUST BE SEAMLESS or you hear the join every few seconds.
Audacity: Effect > Crossfade Loop

Free audio: https://freesound.org  -- CHECK EACH LICENCE.
Configured in: src/config/manifest.js
